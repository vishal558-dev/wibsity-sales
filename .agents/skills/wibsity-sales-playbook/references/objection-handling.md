# Objection Handling Playbook for wibsity

The **A-C-R Method** (Acknowledge, Clarify, Reframe) allows you to turn common SMB hesitations into consultative discovery.

---

## 1. "We already have a website."

- **Psychology**: The owner treats a website as a digital business card, not an active lead generation asset.
- **Acknowledge**: *"Understood, and I actually checked out your existing website before calling."*
- **Clarify & Reframe**: *"The reason I'm reaching out isn't because you don't have a website, but because of how it's performing on mobile. Right now, over 80% of local customers search on smartphones. On your current site, there is no direct tap-to-call or WhatsApp button on screen — meaning visitors have to write down your number or search for a form. Most people simply bounce to the next competitor.*  
*Does your current website consistently bring in 5-10 new inquiries every week?"*

---

## 2. "We get all our business from word-of-mouth / referrals."

- **Psychology**: They take pride in their service quality, which is great. Use that to your advantage.
- **Acknowledge**: *"That speaks volumes about the quality of your work. Referrals are the best clients."*
- **Reframe**: *"Here's what happens today though: when someone gets a referral about {{business_name}}, the very first thing they do before calling is Google your company name. When they land on a slow, outdated site that doesn't work well on mobile, it damages the trust your referral just built. A high-performance website ensures your referral conversions stay as high as possible."*

---

## 3. "We get our leads from Justdial / IndiaMART."

- **Psychology**: They believe portals are the only viable digital channel.
- **Acknowledge**: *"Those platforms are great for immediate volume."*
- **Challenge**: *"The challenge with Justdial and IndiaMART is that every time an inquiry comes in, it gets shared with 4 or 5 of your competitors simultaneously. You end up in a race to the bottom on price. With your own optimized website, every single lead is 100% exclusive to you."*

---

## 4. "It's too expensive / We don't have the budget right now."

- **Psychology**: Price objection is almost always a value or timing objection.
- **Acknowledge**: *"I completely understand being cautious with expenses."*
- **Reframe**: *"What is the average lifetime value of just one new client in your business? [Wait for answer, e.g. ₹50,000 or ₹2,00,000]. Exactly. If a redesigned, fast website brings in just one or two extra clients over the next 12 months, the site completely pays for itself. Everything after that is pure profit."*

---

## 5. "Send details on WhatsApp / Email and I'll review it."

- **Psychology**: Polite brush-off to get you off the phone.
- **Acknowledge & Micro-Commitment**: *"I will happily send it across right now. But so that I don't send you 10 pages of generic info: what is the single biggest thing you'd want your website to do better right now — get more calls, rank better on Google, or look more modern for high-end clients?"*
- **Lock-in**: *"Great, I'll send that specific breakdown to your WhatsApp. Are you around tomorrow at 3 PM for a quick 2-minute follow-up?"*

---

## 6. "Call me back next month / next quarter."

- **Acknowledge**: *"I can certainly do that."*
- **Urgency Hook**: *"Just so you know, right now your mobile site has a technical issue where the contact button isn't clickable. That means every week that passes, you're actively losing inquiries from customers trying to reach you. Let's take just 5 minutes this Thursday to show you what needs fixing, and whenever you're ready to proceed down the road, you'll already have the plan."*
