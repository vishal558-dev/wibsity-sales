# WhatsApp Outreach & Follow-up Playbook for wibsity

WhatsApp is the primary conversational channel for Indian business owners. Keep it casual, concise, and value-packed.

---

## 1. Post-Call Audit Follow-up (Sent immediately after a cold call)

```text
Hi {{contact_name}}, this is Vishal from wibsity (we just spoke on the phone).

As promised, here is the quick audit summary for {{business_name}}'s website:

🔴 Mobile Experience: 35/100 (No tap-to-call button above the fold)
🔴 Load Speed: 4.8s (Causes visitors to drop off)
🟢 SEO Structure: Good basic setup

Attached is the 1-page visual breakdown 📄

Would love to walk you through a 5-minute preview of how we'd fix this to boost your incoming customer calls. Are you free tomorrow around 4 PM?
```

---

## 2. Direct Cold WhatsApp (If phone was unanswered)

```text
Hi {{contact_name}}, hope you're having a good week.

I'm Vishal from wibsity (https://wibsity.in). I was looking at {{business_name}}'s website today and noticed a couple of technical issues on mobile that are causing prospective customers to drop off before contacting you.

Put together a quick 1-page audit screenshot for you.

Can I share it here?
```

---

## 3. Proposal Follow-up / Closing Sequence

### Touch 1 (24 hours after proposal sent):
```text
Hi {{contact_name}}, hope you had a chance to review the website redesign proposal for {{business_name}}.

Any questions on the scope, timeline, or pricing? Happy to jump on a 5-minute call if helpful.
```

### Touch 2 (48 hours later):
```text
Hi {{contact_name}}, just wanted to let you know we have one development slot opening up next week. 

If we finalize the proposal this week, we can have {{business_name}}'s new high-converting website live within 10 business days. Let me know if you'd like to get started!
```
