# Cold Calling Scripts for wibsity

Cold calling SMB founders/owners requires brevity, respect for their time, and an immediate observable hook.

---

## 1. Universal Cold Call Script (Audit-Backed)

**Opener:**
> *"Hi [Prospect Name], this is Vishal from wibsity. I know you weren't expecting my call, so I'll keep this under 45 seconds.*  
> *I was looking at your website [Company Website] while researching [Industry] companies in [City], and I noticed two specific technical issues on your mobile page that are likely preventing visitors from calling you."*

**The Pause:**
> *(Stop talking and wait for them to respond — typically: "What issues?" or "What do you mean?")*

**The Explanation:**
> *"Right now, when someone searches for a [Industry Provider] on their phone and clicks your link:  
> 1. There is no tap-to-call button above the fold, so they have to scroll and copy-paste your number.  
> 2. [Second issue: e.g. The page took over 5 seconds to load / The contact form doesn't display cleanly on mobile].  
> Most potential customers simply bounce back to Google and call your competitor instead."*

**The Value Proposition:**
> *"At wibsity, we redesign and build high-performance business websites that convert everyday visitors into direct phone inquiries and booked jobs."*

**The Low-Friction Close:**
> *"I don't expect you to take my word for it on a cold call. I put together a 1-page visual audit breakdown showing exactly where visitors are dropping off. Can I send that over to your WhatsApp so you can take a look?"*

**If they say yes:**
> *"Great, is this the best WhatsApp number? ... Perfect, I'll send it across in 5 minutes. I'll follow up tomorrow afternoon to see what you thought."*

## 2. Industry Variations

### A. Solar Companies (High ticket, quotation-driven)
- **Pain point**: Potential clients want quotes immediately; long forms or missing solar calculator/quote CTAs lose leads.
- **Hook**: *"I noticed on mobile that to request a site inspection or quote, a customer has to fill out an 8-field form rather than a 1-click WhatsApp quote button. In your space, that cuts inquiries in half."*

### B. Healthcare & Clinics (Appointment-driven)
- **Pain point**: Patients on mobile need instant doctor timings and direct clinic calling.
- **Hook**: *"I noticed that when a patient visits your site on mobile, there's no sticky 'Call Reception' or 'Book Appointment' button. Most patients looking for an appointment won't hunt through an 'About Us' page to find your phone number."*

### C. B2B Services / Manufacturing (Trust & catalog-driven)
- **Pain point**: Slow PDFs, unoptimized catalogs, lack of HTTPS, outdated look.
- **Hook**: *"When overseas or out-of-state buyers look up your product catalog, the site is currently loading over unencrypted HTTP and takes 6+ seconds on mobile. For high-value contracts, that first impression directly impacts your perceived credibility."*

## 3. Gatekeeper Handling

If a receptionist, assistant, or executive answers:

**The Casual Authority Approach:**
> *"Hi, could you please connect me with [Owner's Name] regarding the technical performance issue on [company website]?"*

**If asked what it's about:**
> *"It's about their customer contact link on mobile — it's currently broken for incoming inquiries. Is [Owner] available for a quick moment, or what is his direct extension?"*
